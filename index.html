What to check before you order

LoloBuy finds and product links

For a Weidian product link, Taobao listing or 1688 product page, record the source item ID and selected variant. Match the seller’s color option, size label and listing photos before adding a row. Date each check so a replaced listing or expired product link is easy to spot later.

LoloBuy sizing guide

For shoes, compare the sneaker insole length in centimeters with a pair you wear. For hoodies and jackets, lay a garment flat and measure chest width, shoulder width and sleeve length. Check the waistband, rise and inseam for pants. For a matching set, confirm that the top and bottoms come in the sizes you need.

LoloBuy QC photo checklist

Compare warehouse QC photos against the exact variant you saved. Look for uneven seams, damaged zippers, loose threads and mismatched shoe labels. For a cap, check the head circumference and closure. For jewelry, request a close-up of the clasp and ask for the bracelet’s inner circumference or the earrings’ drop length.

LoloBuy shipping guide

Keep item weight and packed parcel weight in separate fields. Compare parcel dimensions, volumetric weight, shoebox packaging and any parcel consolidation fees. Before shipping to the US, UK or EU, check destination-specific delivery options and whether the estimate includes import charges or local handling fees.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LoloBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list that can record seller links, variants, sizing notes and quality-control photos. Use each row as a starting point and confirm the details on the current seller listing.

Where do the category links go?

All ten category links open CAFANS category pages. This is an independent shopping guide.

Which QC photos should I request for sneakers?

Ask for both shoes together, both heels, both outsoles and the size labels. Request an insole measurement if you need to compare fit with a pair you already own.

Can QC photos confirm a branded item is authentic?

QC photos show visible details and condition. To assess authenticity, check the seller’s provenance and documentation or use a qualified authentication service.

What do I need to compare shipping quotes?

Use your destination, final packed weight, outer parcel dimensions and the shipping line’s billing rules. Check whether the quote includes any applicable taxes or handling charges.
